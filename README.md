# haydenhatch_showcase
Database Design and Implementation followed by Data Analysis in SQL

Project Background:

Eastern Sawtooth College (ESC) is a small junior business school in
Salmon, Idaho. The school offers undergraduate degrees and certifications
in four areas of study: business analytics, accounting, marketing, and
economics. Since the school’s opening three years ago, the registrar has
been tracking information about students, faculty, enrollment, course
offerings, and tuition in an Excel file. This form of data tracking has worked
fine up until now, but, as the school has grown, it has recently begun to
show severe limitations. The data lacks consistent structure, updating the
data is confusing and time-intensive, and analytical capabilities are limited.
The school has hired a database consulting service to help solve its data storage woes.
Your first task in your new position is to use a small subset of the registrar’s Excel 
workbook to design an efficient relational database that can later be used to maintain
enrollment figures, student information, and information about the school’s academic
programs.
